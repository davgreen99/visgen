Process

How it works

01

Upload your track

Drop in any audio file — MP3, WAV, FLAC or AAC, up to 50 MB.

02

The model listens

Your track becomes a mel spectrogram; a picture of how its energy is spread across pitch and time. A network reads it, trained from scratch on more than 2,000 tracks.

03

Nine decisions

The network returns decisions for which formula to draw, mood, energy, valence, arousal colour temperature, complexity, motion and brightness. All of which you can configure with set sliders.

04

Export & share

The exporter re-renders offline on a fixed clock, so the video matches what you saw frame for frame. Encoding happens on your GPU, the audio never leaves your browser.

Features

What the AI sees

〜

Frequency & Spectrum

The live analyser splits playback into bass, mid and treble. Each formula maps those bands onto its own parameters, so the figure moves with what you are actually hearing.

◎

Tempo & Beat Grid

Beats are tracked into a grid before playback starts, and each impulse fires against your position in the track rather than a smoothed level meter, allowing fast music stays crisp instead of lagging.

◇

Valence & Arousal

These two outputs learned from 1,802 tracks rated by human listeners: valence sets hue and lightness; arousal sets saturation.

⬡

Structure & Sections

Self-similarity analysis finds where a track stops resembling itself: intro, breakdown, drop. The figure re-forms at every boundary instead of holding one shape for several minutes.

▲

Formula Choice

The model's style output is the name of a renderer, meaning its prediction is the visual: harmonograph, Chladni plate, de Jong, waveform, Lorenz, Thomas or Aizawa.

✦

Key, Tempo & Timbre

Musical key by chroma correlation, BPM, and timbre brightness from the track's MFCCs, measured on every upload and reported alongside the visual.
